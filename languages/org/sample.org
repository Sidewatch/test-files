* Overview
The inventory service keeps stock levels for every warehouse.
Text can be *bold*, /italic/, _underlined_, +strikethrough+, =verbatim=, ~code~,
and mix *bold with /italic/ inside*. Subscripts: H_2O, x^2, x_{i+1}, a^{b+c}.
A line break forces\\
a new line. Special symbols: \alpha \beta \rightarrow \le \ge \pi \deg, and --- dashes -- too.
